# Code of Conduct

Digital GiGz AI Lab is committed to a respectful, inclusive, and
evidence-minded community.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, specific, and constructive.
- Welcome different levels of technical and healthcare experience.
- Respect privacy, consent, professional boundaries, and local law.
- Challenge claims and ideas without attacking people.
- Acknowledge uncertainty and correct mistakes transparently.
- Make participation accessible wherever practical.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks
- Sharing another person’s private or health information
- Fabricating evidence, credentials, affiliations, or endorsements
- Promoting unsafe clinical use or presenting educational material as medical
  advice
- Deliberate disruption, spam, or security abuse

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to `hello@digitalgigz.ai`. Include the relevant
context without forwarding unnecessary personal or sensitive information.
Project maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take other
proportionate action to protect the community.
